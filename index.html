Alyssa

I am a senior at the University of Texas at Dallas, majoring in Arts, Technology, and Emerging Communication. I am graduating this semester!

My work focuses mainly on nature and still-image photography. I use the camera to slow down and highlight the quiet details, subtle changes, and ordinary moments that can otherwise go unnoticed.
